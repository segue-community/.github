# SEGUE: A community of urban scholars collaborating to update segregation computation with R
[![](https://img.shields.io/static/v1?label=Rbanism&message=OER&labelColor=93278F&color=00A99D&style=flat-square)](https://github.com/Rbanism)

## Who are we?

We are a diverse and interdisciplinary community of [25 scholars](https://github.com/orgs/segue-community/people) working together to update segregation computation with R by:
- fostering research conversations on the state of segregation measurement and its relation to theories, data, scale and social attributes considered;
- developing a new R package to assist with segregation measure selection, computation, visualization and/or analysis.
- collaborating on comparative research, theory and methodology development.

This community was brought together for the 2026 [workshop "Updating urban segregation computation with R"](https://erc-segue.nl/Workshop), organised by [Rbanism](https://github.com/Rbanism), funded by the [ERC project SEGUE](https://github.com/SEGUE-Project) and led by [Clémentine Cottineau-Mugadza](https://github.com/ClementineCttn). The workshop took place on 9-13th November 2026 at TU Delft, in the Netherlands. During this workshop, we agreed:

1. to build a new R package to ease the selection, computation and visualisation of segregation, as a community.
2. to avoid the use of AI and rather make use of our own collectively diverse skillsets and social networks.
3. to organise the design of this package around multiple sub-groups (with overlapping topics and contributors): the **Stewardship** group lead by [Timothée Giraud](https://github.com/rCarto), the **Routing** group led by [Selcan Mutgan](https://github.com/selcanm), the **Visualisation** group led by [Claudiu Forgaci](https://github.com/cforgaci), the **Extra indicators** group led by [Javier San Millán Tejedor](https://github.com/javiersanmillan), the **Integrated framework** group led by [Ignacio Urria Yañez](https://github.com/iurriayanez) and the **coordination** group, led by [Clémentine Cottineau-Mugadza](https://github.com/ClementineCttn).
4. to develop this new R package as an open-source project.
5. to aim for a draft version of the package in 2027.

## Community code of conduct
- Please use friendly, respectful and inclusive language. 
- Be respectful of different opinions, skills and experiences. 
- Please frame your feedback as constructive criticism, and receive it as such.
- Focus on what is best for the community. 
- Avoid unacceptable behaviours, ask others to stop unacceptable behaviours when you spot them and report unacceptable behaviours. These include written or verbal violence, threats, intimidation, discrimination, persistence disruption, the display of inadequate images or the publication of private communication without consent. You can report these to any of the SEGUE package core team members (i.e. Clémentine, Claudiu and Ignacio).
